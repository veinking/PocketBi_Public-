# Community Conduct

The PocketBI public repository is intended to be a useful place for documentation, product feedback, reproducible bug reports, and constructive discussion.

## Expected behavior

Please:

- communicate respectfully
- focus criticism on the product, documentation, or technical behavior rather than people
- provide reproducible details when reporting bugs
- use synthetic or sanitized data in public examples
- respect privacy and intellectual property
- follow the security-reporting process for sensitive issues
- assume other contributors may have different experience levels

## Not acceptable

This repository is not a place for:

- harassment or threats
- discriminatory or demeaning language
- spam
- publishing another person's private information
- posting credentials, customer data, or confidential datasets
- deliberately publishing exploit details for an unresolved vulnerability
- impersonation
- intentionally disruptive behavior

## Technical disagreements

Technical disagreement is welcome. Be specific about:

- observed behavior
- expected behavior
- evidence
- tradeoffs
- reproducibility

Strong criticism of a design or implementation is fine when it remains about the work.

## Security and privacy

If a discussion starts to involve credentials, unauthorized access, private data, or an exploitable security condition, stop posting details publicly and follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that materially violates these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect users or keep the repository usable.

Questions can be directed to **support@pocketbi.app**.
